## Pricing Estimate

Planning estimate only, not a fixed vendor quote. All amounts are in INR and exclude GST unless noted. Estimates assume an India-first launch, English, Kannada, and Hindi at launch, a shared-codebase Android/iOS app, a responsive web app, an admin portal, existing content ready for cataloguing, and approximately 1,000-5,000 monthly active users in the initial hosting period.

### One-Time App Development

| Work area | Estimated cost |
|---|---:|
| Product definition and UX/UI design | Rs. 1.5-2.5 lakh |
| Android and iOS apps using a shared codebase | Rs. 8-12 lakh |
| Responsive web app | Rs. 3-5 lakh |
| Backend, database, and content/admin portal | Rs. 5-8 lakh |
| OTP login, subscriptions, and payment integration | Rs. 3-6 lakh |
| Multilanguage engineering and language QA | Rs. 1.5-3 lakh |
| Testing, release preparation, and store submission | Rs. 2-4 lakh |
| **Estimated development total** | **Rs. 24-41 lakh** |

Multilanguage engineering includes language selection, translated interface text, localized content descriptions, language-aware search, and support for subtitles or alternate audio tracks. It does not include translating or dubbing the existing video library.

### One-Time Launch Expenses

| Expense | Estimated cost |
|---|---:|
| Translation of interface text and a modest content catalogue in three languages | Rs. 1-4 lakh |
| Legal review of privacy policy, terms, and subscription wording | Rs. 50,000-2 lakh |
| Independent security testing | Rs. 75,000-2 lakh |
| Domain and business email setup | Rs. 2,000-10,000 per year |
| Apple Developer Program membership | US$99 per year; local price, taxes, and currency conversion may vary |
| Google Play developer account | One-time registration fee; confirm the current amount during signup |

Apple states that qualifying nonprofits may request a developer-program fee waiver. Subtitle translation, voiceover, dubbing, and video production are not included; their cost depends on the number and length of videos and the target languages.

### Monthly Hosting and Operations

| Expense | Early-stage estimate |
|---|---:|
| Backend, database, backups, logs, and monitoring | Rs. 5,000-20,000 per month |
| Premium video hosting and streaming | Rs. 5,000-50,000+ per month |
| Maintenance, bug fixes, security/dependency updates, and app-store compatibility | Rs. 50,000-1.2 lakh per month |
| OTP SMS | Usage-based; depends on provider, delivery, and resend rate |
| **Estimated recurring total** | **Rs. 60,000-1.9 lakh+ per month** |

Video delivery is usage-sensitive; viewing hours and bandwidth can increase the monthly bill as the audience grows. Free YouTube embeds can reduce video-hosting costs, but YouTube policy prohibits charging users to watch videos in an embedded player. Use YouTube for free content and a separate licensed video service for premium content.

### Payment and Store Fees

- Razorpay currently lists a standard domestic fee of 2% per successful transaction, plus 18% GST on its fee. As an example, Rs. 1 lakh in successful web payments would incur about Rs. 2,360 at that rate. Verify the provider's current rate before launch.
- Mobile-store billing fees are separate and depend on the platform program, location, and eligibility. Budget for Apple In-App Purchase and Google Play Billing for digital subscriptions, then confirm the applicable terms before implementation.
- OTP, video streaming, and payment fees are variable and are not included in the app-development total.

### Overall Planning Budget

| Budget period | Rough estimate |
|---|---:|
| App development | Rs. 24-41 lakh one time |
| Launch extras: initial translation, legal review, and security testing | Rs. 2.25-8 lakh one time |

The first-year total excludes GST, payment/store commissions, OTP usage, significant video translation or dubbing, and new features such as offline downloads or live classes. A reasonable initial delivery timeline is 12-16 weeks, subject to timely content and product decisions.
