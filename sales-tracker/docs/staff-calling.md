# Staff calling queue

The staff landing page shows the **Calling Queue**, a search field, and full-width contact cards sized to the available screen. Each contact card includes the business details, follow-up date when present, and buttons to view history or dial/update the lead.

Use the visible **Contacts · [selected queue]** button to slide open the contact menu. Choose **To Call**, **Follow-up**, one of the four call action items, **Orders**, or **All** to switch queues; the menu closes after selection. The lead count beside the button reflects the current queue and search.

On a contact card, choose **Dial / Update** to open the call workflow or **History** to view previous interactions.

The contact cards are shown as full-width tiles in a vertical list, alternating muted blue, indigo, and gray shades with square corners and consistent borders in both themes. In the call form, selected action and outcome buttons are clearly highlighted in indigo and violet, separate from the green call/save controls. Select the **Contacts · [selected queue]** section heading to collapse or expand the list; it starts expanded.

Use the app's theme toggle to switch between light mode and the dark appearance. The alternating contact tiles use deeper blue, indigo, and gray shades in dark mode, and the selected theme is remembered by the browser.

## Activity calendar

Expand **Activity calendar** to review your logged calls and scheduled follow-ups. Choose **Today**, **Date**, or **Date range**, then use **Filter by metric** to show all activity, calls, scheduled follow-ups, one of the four action items, or one of the six outcomes. The calendar is scoped to your account. Call entries use the time the call was logged; scheduled follow-up entries use the follow-up date/time saved with the call.
